COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bridgeway36 memberships and access.

What is the difference between the $25 Annual Pass and $100 Lifetime? +
The Annual Pass ($25/year) provides full unmetered reading access, assessments, and regular monthly releases for one full year. The Lifetime All-Access Pass ($100 one-time) gives you permanent lifetime ownership with zero renewals ever, including all future books, GenAI course pathways, and priority author briefings.
Can our organization purchase group licenses for our workforce? +
Yes. We offer central billing, volume seat discounts, and organizational cohort analytics for corporate departments, non-profits, and educational institutions. Contact licensing@bridgeway36.com for tailored quotes.
What reading formats are supported in the eLibrary? +
All Bridgeway36 publications are available for high-fidelity interactive online reading in any modern browser, as well as downloadable, DRM-free PDF and ePub editions for Kindle, iPad, and e-readers.
How does the Transition Readiness Index™ (TRI) assessment work? +
The TRI assessment measures five core cognitive agility dimensions: Cognitive Flexibility, Role Detachment, Strategic Literacy, Network Agility, and Purpose Anchoring. It generates a personalized reflection report and recommended reading pathway.
